# Dataset Description
The dataset originates from a fictional retail company — Superstore — operating across various regions in the United States. It contains detailed sales transaction records over multiple years, focusing on customer orders, product information, shipping details, and financial performance metrics.

# Tool used
Power BI , PPT

# Insights

### Here are some insights from the sales dashboard:

**📊 Overall Performance**
Total Sales: 2.26 million

Total Profit: 279.09K

The profit margin appears to be approximately 12.35%, suggesting moderate profitability.

**🧭 Sales by Region**
West is the top-performing region with 0.71M in sales.

East follows closely at 0.67M.

Central and South lag behind with 0.49M and 0.39M, respectively.

Insight: Consider deeper market penetration strategies for Central and South regions.

**🗂️ Sales by Category**
Technology leads with 36.62% of total sales.

Furniture accounts for 32.16%.

Office Supplies contribute 31.22%.

Insight: Technology is the dominant category and likely the most profitable — could be prioritized in marketing efforts.

**📆 Sales Trend by Month**
Sales peak in November (~0.35M) and decline steadily through to February (lowest < 0.1M).

This indicates strong Q4 performance (possibly due to holiday season).

Insight: Consider promotional strategies to boost sales in Q1 and Q2 where performance is weaker.

**✅ Actionable Recommendations**
Boost Q1-Q2 Sales: Launch campaigns or discounts during low-performing months like January and February.

Focus on Central & South Regions: Investigate reasons for underperformance and localize strategies.

Invest in Technology Category: It shows strong sales potential — consider bundling or cross-selling with other categories.

Monitor Profitability: While sales are strong, the profit margin could be improved by controlling costs or focusing on higher-margin products.
